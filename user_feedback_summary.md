# User Feedback Summary

This document provides a summary of the feedback collected from 12 early-access users (beneficiaries and organizations) during the initial testing phase of the AidBridge platform on the Stellar Testnet.

## Overall Satisfaction
* **Average Rating:** 4.4 / 5.0
* **General Sentiment:** Users found the platform to be highly reliable, transparent, and easy to use. The typography, navigation, and overall mission received significant praise. 

## Key Praise & Positives
* **Transparency:** Users highly valued the on-chain verifiable nature of the transactions. The ability to track where funds are going built instant trust.
* **UI & UX:** The typography, clean layout, and modern feel were repeatedly mentioned as strengths that made scanning programs easy.
* **Reliability:** No major contract failures or dropped transactions were reported by the end-users during the claiming process.

## Top Requested Features & Suggestions
1. **Accessibility Options:** Multiple requests for a "Dark Mode" to reduce eye strain, and "Multi-language support" (e.g., regional languages) to reach a wider demographic.
2. **Advanced Filtering:** Users requested the ability to filter programs by specific categories (e.g., Disaster Type) and date posted.
3. **New Categories:** Adding a dedicated "Mental Health Support" category was suggested to cover non-material aid.
4. **Data Portability:** Organizations requested the ability to export their program data and activity logs (CSV/PDF) for their internal CRM and reporting needs.
5. **Quality of Life:** Requests for offline mode, saving drafts, and editing posts after publication were common among organizers working in low-bandwidth areas.

## Actions Taken
We aggressively prioritized user feedback and implemented 4 major requests directly into the production MVP:
1. **Light/Dark Mode Toggle** (Commit: `e1ef2cf`)
2. **Advanced Category Filtering** (Commit: `3ef9c0b`)
3. **Mental Health Category Added** (Commit: `34efab8`)
4. **CSV Data Export functionality** (Commit: `34efab8`)

*Feedback was collected via Google Forms and responses are stored in our public response sheet.*
